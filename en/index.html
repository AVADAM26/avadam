Our story

Why AVADAM?

AVADAM is made from our names, Adrian von Arb and Daniel Muccio. You say it like Adam.

And that is no accident. Adam stands for the first human being. That is where it all began, including the age of AI. Without people there would be no robotics, no AI and no technology.

So AVADAM is more than two names and a company. It is meant to remind us where we come from.

FAQ

What people often ask. Answered honestly.

Your question isn't here? Drop us a line, no strings attached.

Can I edit my website myself?

Not through a login, and that's on purpose. Without a CMS there are no updates, no plugins and nothing that breaks overnight. You send us your changes, we make them and charge for the time. No subscription.

Why not WordPress or a website builder?

For a site that shows what you do, that's too much machinery. Constant updates, security holes, monthly fees. Built by hand, it loads faster, is safer and, after a one-off setup cost, only costs hosting and a domain to run.

How long until it goes live?

Usually around two months from the go-ahead. Texts and pictures take the most time, not the build. If you have a fixed date, tell us right at the start and we'll be honest about whether it's doable.

Can you also take photos or videos?

Yes, Adrian is a passionate photographer and filmmaker. If good pictures are missing, you can book this as an extra, on site with you, at your business for example. That way pictures, videos and website fit together perfectly, all from one place.

Will people find me on Google?

The site is built so Google can read it well, with a clean structure, fast loading and the right details in the background. Nobody can guarantee you the top spot, honestly. For local searches your Google Business Profile matters too. We're happy to help, but you'll need to set it up yourself.
